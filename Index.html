About

I am a Senior Lecturer in the Department of Electronics, Communication and IT at Kathmandu Engineering College, Tribhuvan University — a role I have held since 2076 B.S.

My academic background spans a Master's in Information System Engineering (Purvanchal University, GPA 3.67) and a Bachelor's in Electronics and Communication Engineering (Tribhuvan University, 76.77%).

Before academia, I worked as an Electronics Engineer at Clean Power Pvt. Ltd., where I designed and fabricated inverters, UPS systems, and stabilisers — experience that grounds my teaching in real-world practice.
